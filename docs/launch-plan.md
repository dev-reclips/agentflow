## Priority 1: Hacker News — Show HN

**Time to execute:** 30 minutes  
**Audience:** Developers, technical founders, CTOs  
**Cost:** Free  
**Best time to post:** Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am EST

### The post

**Title:** `Show HN: AgentFlow – autonomous AI agents that work your GitHub backlog`

**Body:**
```
I built AgentFlow after getting tired of watching 60% of a team's GitHub issues 
sit unresolved for weeks while everyone was heads-down on "priority" work.

The idea: what if you could assign a GitHub issue to an AI agent the same way 
you assign it to an engineer? The agent triages it, writes the code, opens a PR, 
and closes the ticket. You review and merge.

It works best for: bug fixes with clear reproduction steps, dependency bumps, 
documentation gaps, test coverage, and small well-specced features.

14-day free trial, no credit card. Takes 10 minutes to connect your first repo.
Happy to answer anything about how it works under the hood.
```

**URL:** https://agentflow.ai

**What to expect:**
- 200–2,000 views depending on upvotes
- 5–20 trial sign-ups if it lands on page 1
- Good comments are free user research

**How to get upvotes:** Share the HN link with 5 people who use HN and ask them to upvote if they find it interesting. Do not ask people to upvote indiscriminately — HN detects vote rings.
