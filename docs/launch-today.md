## Fastest path to first revenue: warm network (15 minutes)

Before cold email or social media, reach out to 3 people you already know who run engineering teams.

**Who to think of:**
- Former colleagues who became CTOs or VPs Eng
- Founders you've advised or met at events
- People who have posted on LinkedIn about "technical debt" or "hiring engineers"

**Script (Slack/iMessage/LinkedIn DM — not email):**

> Hey [Name] — building something I think you'd find useful. We just launched AgentFlow: AI agents that work your GitHub backlog automatically. Assign an issue to an agent like you'd assign it to an engineer — it writes the code, opens a PR, closes the ticket. 14-day free trial, no credit card.
>
> Would you try it on a real backlog issue? Takes 10 minutes to connect a repo.
> https://agentflow.ai
>
> If it's useful, I'd love 15 minutes of feedback.

Send to 3 people. That's it. One yes from a warm contact can close within a week.
