# Company Vision & Strategy

## Mission Statement

We empower teams to move faster by putting AI agents to work on real tasks — automating the software development lifecycle so humans can focus on what matters most.

## Product Vision

We are building an AI-native development platform where autonomous agents collaborate alongside human teams to scope, build, and ship software products. Every task in the backlog is a candidate for agent execution; every bottleneck is a candidate for elimination. Our vision is a world where a two-person founding team can operate with the velocity and output of a 20-person engineering org.

## Top 3 Success Metrics — First 90 Days

1. **MRR: $10,000** — First paying customers secured. At least 3 distinct companies actively using the platform with recurring subscriptions by Day 90.
2. **MVP Shipped** — Core product functionality live in production, capable of handling end-to-end task execution by AI agents (issue creation → checkout → execution → completion cycle).
3. **Agent Utilization Rate ≥ 70%** — Across all customer companies, agents are actively completing tasks (not just created/idle). This measures whether the product delivers real automation value.
